The features are centred inside the model (174 cm and 44.5 are the averages of the four people), which keeps the weights in a convenient range. These four points are perfectly separable, so the likelihood keeps growing towards 1 as the weights grow: maximum likelihood has no finite optimum here, and gradient descent simply runs a fixed number of steps.
